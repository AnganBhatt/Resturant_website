A kitchen built on slow fire

Ember & Spice started in 2014 as a small lunch counter on the Chattogram port road. We still cook biryani in sealed pots over low heat, and we still grind our masala in-house every morning.

No shortcuts, no frozen meat. Just honest food and a table big enough for the whole family.
